A few things worth knowing

Can guests contribute without buying the app?

Yes. The organiser gives each guest or helper an individual invitation link. Permissions depend on the invitation: RSVP and gifts, assigned tasks, or gallery viewing.

Does it automatically track Amazon purchases?

Not in this edition. You can link to Amazon and other gift registries and products. Guests record their purchase in BigDaySorted. Purchases made elsewhere are not automatically verified or synchronised.

Can the couple see gift choices early?

BigDaySorted hides reservations and purchaser details from organisers and honourees until the protected reveal time after the event. External retailers may show their own purchase information; their websites are outside our control.

Are invitations and thank-you emails sent automatically?

This edition creates shareable invitation links and personalised drafts. You send them using your own email service. Automatic delivery will require a configured email provider.

What is available for testing?

Event creation, timelines, assigned task updates, suppliers and budgets, RSVP invitations, gift reservations, moderated galleries, document uploads and post-event thank-you drafts. Paid licences, native store downloads, advanced seating plans, payments and ticketing are not enabled.
